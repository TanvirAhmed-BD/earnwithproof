Article Title Here — Question Style Works Best

One or two sentences that answer the reader’s question up front and say what proof the article shows (a timeline, a fee breakdown, a screenshot). Published Month Year · by Tanvir Ahmed.

Key takeaways

  • The single most useful fact, with the real number.
  • The step or rule that matters most.
  • The honest caveat — what did not work, or what it really cost.

First Main Section

Body text. Lead with first-hand experience: what was done, in what order, and what happened. Use exact figures from real platform rates or receipts wherever a number appears.

Second Main Section

Body text. Screenshots, tables and worked examples belong here — the proof that separates this article from generic advice.

Third Main Section

Body text. Include the mistakes and the honest limits of the method. If the method earned nothing, say so plainly.

Bottom Line

A short, honest summary: who this method suits, who should skip it, and the realistic expectation to set.
