How Do I Withdraw My Fiverr Earnings in Bangladesh Without PayPal? (Payoneer to Bank/bKash, Step by Step)

The short answer: Fiverr → Payoneer → your Bangladeshi bank account, and from there to bKash through your bank’s app if you want it in mobile money. You do not need PayPal to withdraw Fiverr earnings in Bangladesh — and for receiving money here, PayPal is not really an option anyway. Below is the exact route I have set up and verified on my own accounts, every fee on the path, and one honest caveat up front: my first order has not landed yet, so this is my tested setup and the published fee maths, not a screenshot of a completed withdrawal. I will update this page with the real receipt when it exists. Published November 2026 · by Tanvir Ahmed.

Key takeaways

  • PayPal does not support receiving and withdrawing money in Bangladesh — Payoneer is the standard route for Fiverr sellers here, and it is already built into Fiverr’s withdrawal options.
  • The path has four stops: cleared Fiverr balance, Payoneer, your local bank in taka, then bKash via your bank app if you need mobile money.
  • On a $100 order, Fiverr’s 20% fee, the withdrawal fee and Payoneer’s conversion margin leave you roughly $74 worth of taka — plan for the full fee stack, not the headline price.
  • Your name must match exactly across Fiverr, Payoneer and your bank account, or withdrawals get stuck.

Why PayPal Is Not the Answer in Bangladesh

Almost every “how to withdraw” guide on the internet assumes you have PayPal. If you are in Bangladesh, you do not — at least not in a form that helps. PayPal does not let Bangladesh-based users receive payments and withdraw them to a local bank account, so a Fiverr balance paid out “to PayPal” would be money you cannot properly touch. That is also why you should never pay one of the unofficial “dollar buyers” who offer to convert balances for you: handing account access or balance transfers to a stranger is how sellers lose both the money and the account.

The good news is that Fiverr knows its sellers are global. Alongside PayPal, Fiverr offers withdrawal through Payoneer — and its “bank transfer” option runs on Payoneer’s rails too. Payoneer works in Bangladesh, verifies Bangladeshi ID, and pays out to Bangladeshi bank accounts in taka. For a seller here, it is not a workaround; it is the main road.

How to Withdraw Fiverr Earnings in Bangladesh: The Route at a Glance

Here is the whole journey before we slow down for each step:

  1. Your order completes and Fiverr takes its 20% fee. The rest sits in “pending clearance.”
  2. After the clearance period, the money becomes “available for withdrawal” in your Fiverr earnings page.
  3. You withdraw from Fiverr to your linked Payoneer account (USD).
  4. You withdraw from Payoneer to your Bangladeshi bank account, where it arrives converted to taka.
  5. Optional: move money from your bank to bKash using your bank’s app.

That is the setup I have in place: my Payoneer account is identity-verified in my own name, linked to my Fiverr account, and my Bangladeshi bank account is added to Payoneer ready to receive. The plumbing is done before the first order, deliberately — payment problems are much easier to fix when no money is waiting on them.

Step 1: Wait for Your Balance to Clear (the 20% Fee and the 14 Days)

Two things happen before a single taka can move. First, Fiverr keeps 20% of every order: a buyer pays $100, and $80 is what enters your balance. That is not a withdrawal fee — it is Fiverr’s cut of the sale, taken at the moment the order completes. Second, the $80 does not become withdrawable immediately. For new sellers, funds clear 14 days after the order is marked complete (top-rated sellers wait 7). During those two weeks the money shows as “pending clearance,” and there is no legitimate way to speed it up.

When the period ends, the amount moves to “available for withdrawal” on your Earnings page. That available balance — not your lifetime earnings figure — is what you can actually send to Payoneer. If you want the complete fee-by-fee version of this maths, I work a full $100 order through every stage in How Much Does Fiverr Really Take? A $100 Order Worked Out Fee by Fee.

Step 2: Link Payoneer to Your Fiverr Account

Open Payoneer first and finish identity verification there — account in your own name, your own ID, your own bank details. Then, inside Fiverr, go to your Earnings page, choose the withdrawal method, and select Payoneer; Fiverr walks you through connecting the two accounts. (Fiverr’s direct “Bank Transfer” option is also powered by Payoneer and lands in the same place.)

The detail that matters most is boring: the name on your Payoneer account must match the name on your Fiverr account and the name on your bank account. Mismatched names are the most common reason withdrawals stall or get reviewed. Do not use a relative’s Payoneer, and do not use an account opened with different documents “to save time” — it costs far more time later. If you want the deeper explainer on fees and exchange rates inside Payoneer itself, that is covered in How Does Payoneer Work for Freelancers?

Step 3: Withdraw From Fiverr to Payoneer

From the Earnings page, hit withdraw, choose Payoneer, enter the amount, and confirm. Budget for a $3 withdrawal fee at this stage of the journey — that is the figure used in the published fee schedules this guide’s worked example is built on, and it is why withdrawing small amounts again and again is a bad habit: three $30 withdrawals pay the fee three times, one $90 withdrawal pays it once.

Timing: Fiverr-to-Payoneer transfers typically land within one to three business days. The money arrives in Payoneer as a USD balance. It is “in your account,” but it is not yet in Bangladesh, and it is not yet taka — that is the next step.

Step 4: Move the Money From Payoneer to Your Bangladeshi Bank

In Payoneer, add your local bank account (a BDT account in your own name) if you have not already, then choose Withdraw → To bank account, enter the amount, and confirm. Payoneer converts your dollars to taka at its own exchange rate, which sits a few percent below the mid-market rate you see on Google — roughly a 2% margin on this route. That margin is a real cost, and pretending it does not exist is how sellers end up confused about where money went. There is no separate line on the receipt that says “conversion fee”; the cost is inside the rate.

Bank withdrawals typically take two to five business days to appear in your Bangladeshi account. When the deposit lands, it lands in taka, in your name, in a normal bank account — money you can document, save, or spend like any other income.

Step 5 (Optional): From Your Bank to bKash

One point of confusion to clear up: Payoneer pays banks, not bKash directly. There is no Payoneer-to-bKash button. The “bKash” part of this route happens after the bank deposit: most Bangladeshi banks let you send money to a bKash wallet from their mobile app, usually instantly and for a small bank-side fee. So the honest description of the full path is Fiverr → Payoneer → bank → bKash — two platforms, one bank, and your phone. If anyone offers you a shortcut that skips the bank, they are offering you a risk, not a shortcut.

What a $100 Order Actually Becomes (Worked Example)

This is a worked example built from published fee schedules — my own first withdrawal has not happened yet, and when it does, this table gets replaced with my real figures and the date. Until then, here is the honest preview for a $100 order:

  • Buyer pays: $100.00
  • After Fiverr’s 20% fee: $80.00 (clears after 14 days for a new seller)
  • After the $3 withdrawal fee: $77.00 in Payoneer
  • After Payoneer’s conversion margin (~2%): ≈ $73.72 worth of taka
  • In taka, at an example rate of ≈ ৳120 per dollar: roughly ৳8,850 in your bank (your rate on the day decides the real figure)

Mistakes That Get Withdrawals Stuck

Most withdrawal horror stories are self-inflicted. The four I see repeated:

  • Name mismatch. Fiverr, Payoneer and bank names differ — payments get held for review.
  • Someone else’s account. A cousin’s Payoneer “just for the first payment” can get both accounts flagged.
  • Unofficial dollar buyers. You send balance access, they send promises. Fiverr bans it and the money is unrecoverable.
  • Tiny, frequent withdrawals. Fixed fees punish small withdrawals. Let the balance grow, withdraw once.

The Honest Baseline

One reality check before the bottom line, because this site puts it in every money article: Bankrate’s June 2025 survey (2,616 US adults) found side-hustle participation at 27%, median earnings of $200/month, and only 14% of side hustlers earning over $1,000/month. Freelancing income starts small for almost everyone. That is exactly why the payment route matters from day one — when the amounts are small, losing a quarter of them to confusion, avoidable fees or a frozen account hurts most. My own status, plainly: four Fiverr gigs live, Payoneer verified and linked, first order still to come. The route is ready; now the work has to fill it.

Bottom Line

If you sell on Fiverr from Bangladesh, you do not need PayPal and you do not need anyone’s account but your own. Verify Payoneer in your name, link it to Fiverr, add your bank, and let each balance clear and accumulate before you withdraw. Set the plumbing up before your first order — it takes an afternoon, and it means the best day (the day the first payment clears) is not ruined by paperwork.
