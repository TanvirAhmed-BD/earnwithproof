About EarnWithProof

EarnWithProof is a small, independent site about earning online in practical ways: AI tools and automation, freelancing, digital products, self-publishing and the SEO that helps people find your work. It exists for one reason — most content in this space is written by people who have never actually done the work. This site is written from the work itself. The name is the standard: if a guide says something works, it shows the proof — a screenshot, a fee receipt, a real timeline — instead of asking you to take a guru’s word for it.

About the author

Tanvir Ahmed is a freelancer and self-published author from Bangladesh. He is building online income with zero upfront capital, step by step: offering services on Fiverr, publishing books on Amazon KDP, and selling digital products through platforms that can pay a seller in Bangladesh without a PayPal or Stripe account of his own.

That last detail shapes a lot of what you will read here. Payment plumbing — which platform pays where, what the fees are, how money actually reaches a bank account — is where most “make money online” content goes quiet. EarnWithProof covers it openly, with real numbers from real payouts and fee receipts.

How content here is written

Every guide starts from something the author has personally done, tested or paid for. Timelines are real timelines. Fee examples are worked from actual platform rates. When something failed or earned nothing, the articles say so — a method that produced no income is still useful information if it saves you the same month of work.

What you will never find here: income screenshots used as promises, “get rich quick” claims, or advice presented as financial expertise. This site shares one person’s documented experience, not professional financial advice.

Who this site is for

Beginners and early-stage earners in English-speaking countries — the US, UK, EU and beyond — who want a clear-eyed view of how online income methods actually work before investing their time or money.
